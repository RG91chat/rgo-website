Richard Gaignon

From ceramic engineering to entrepreneurship. More than 30 years of industrial experience.

Trained as an engineer, I spent close to twenty years in ceramic engineering and international industrial projects before acquiring CeramPilot with Christophe Chaput in 2009, later becoming 3DCeram.

My career has taken me from traditional ceramics to technical ceramics and additive manufacturing, and from engineering to sales, from sales to entrepreneurship, and from entrepreneurship to company leadership.

RGO was created to pass that experience on to other entrepreneurs — including the successes, the mistakes and the difficult periods.

Built for the long run
